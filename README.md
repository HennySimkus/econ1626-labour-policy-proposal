### Policy Context

This submission responds to the 2026 Joint Select Committee on Artificial Intelligence inquiry, which examines AI’s effects on productivity, workforce skills, job displacement, job quality, financial services adoption and regulatory gaps. It recommends a two year pilot **Autonomous AI Workforce Transition Scheme** for large regulated financial firms deploying high autonomy AI in material business functions. The scheme would require workforce transition plans and standardised reporting, supported by proportionate firm contributions to a dedicated worker transition fund (Parliament of Australia 2026).
